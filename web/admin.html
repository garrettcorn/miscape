Invite friends

How joining works: your friend installs Lanternfall, runs lanternfall join, and pastes the invite line below. It connects to your computer through an encrypted Tailcat tunnel (peer-to-peer when possible, otherwise through a relay) and opens the game in their browser.
Treat invite lines like a house key: share them privately (direct message), never in public posts or streams.

World settings

Mods are data-only packs (items, recipes, quests, small rule tweaks). They can't run code, read files or use the network. A world that has ever used mods is permanently marked modded; its characters were always private to this world anyway. Mod changes apply after restarting the world.

Saves & backups

Snapshots are written atomically and checksummed; the last 10 are kept in backups/. To restore, stop the world and run lanternfall restore <backup-name>.
